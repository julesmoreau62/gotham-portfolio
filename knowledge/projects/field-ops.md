# ASI Tournament — field operations (id: field-ops)
ASI Multisports Tournament, UFR3S Lille, December 2025. My role: Comms Chief, communication lead and field operations. 500+ people, four disciplines: disc golf, wheelchair basketball, laser tag, spikeball.

- Incident H-2h, facility conflict (critical): the administration double-booked the main gymnasium 2 hours before the start. Alternate gymnasium secured in 45 minutes, equipment transfer coordinated, zero participant impact, on-time start.
- Incident H-30min, weather hazard: heavy rain compromised the outdoor disc golf area. Indoor fallback in 30 minutes, modified course layout, full competition maintained with adapted rules.
- Assets produced: graphic charter (palette + type system), bilingual FR/EN participant briefing and rules, campus and social media poster, event photography and body-cam coverage.
- Gear: Sony α6400, GoPro ×2.
- Takeaway: an event works when people, information and timing work together.
