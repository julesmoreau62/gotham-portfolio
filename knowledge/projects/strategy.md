# BLAST Strategy — "David vs Goliath 2.0" (id: strategy)
Academic case study, 2025. My role: strategic analyst. A 23-page strategic audit of BLAST (esports tournament organiser, Copenhagen) facing the Saudi PIF-backed EFG.

- Situation: BLAST $11M cash vs EFG $1.5Bn valuation. FY24 revenue $82.1M (+13%), net result −$3.96M, gross margin 15.7%. Valve's 2025 rules end franchised partner leagues, collapsing the "Louvre Agreement" barrier.
- Frameworks: PESTEL, VRIO, SWOT.
- Recommendation: move from "boutique" to "mass premium" through a joint venture with Reliance and a mobile-first ecosystem for India's ~600M gamers (market CAGR 19%, $9.2Bn by 2029, JioGames distribution, much lower production costs).
- Roadmap: Phase 1 (H1 2026) India launch: Mumbai studio, Jio/Paytm integration, mobile test event. Phase 2 (H2 2026) monetisation: Fantasy Live, Watch-to-Earn, ARPU +50%. Phase 3 (2027) break-even, revenue mix EU 60% / India 20% / MENA 20%.
- Differentiation: a "Brand Safety Shield" to attract Western sponsors bound by CSRD, away from EFG's PIF-linked reputational risk.
- Extends my M1 thesis on LEC/GRP vs CS2 economic models (interview with BLAST VP James Woollard).
