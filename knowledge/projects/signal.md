# ASN95 Signal — a club's digital presence built from scratch (id: signal)
AS Nortkerque 95, football club founded in 1995, Pas-de-Calais. My role: Head of Communications, Jan → Jun 2025 (bachelor internship).

- Starting point: no content calendar, no visual guidelines, no recurring formats. Sponsors had zero visibility beyond pitch-side banners. The club had a community, but no voice.
- Act I, activation: "Les Pronos du Sultan" with Sultan Kebab, a weekly prediction game (Google Form + manual leaderboard, free meal for the top 3). +467% relative growth in sponsor click-through rate. A V2, "ASN95 Predict" (beta, ~80% done): live rankings, user profiles, sponsor-ready data capture. That was my first app concept.
- Act II, communication: match posters, result graphics, interview series. 15+ visuals per season, 3 recurring formats, 5+ sponsors per visual. The visual language was built week by week until templates were locked mid-season.
- Act III, field: 33 events covered, 500+ shots per match, ~50 delivered per session, 1,650+ edited photos. 26 league matches, 4 youth training camps, 2 general assemblies, the club's 30th anniversary. Youth academy sponsor photoshoot with 50+ kids in branded kits.
- Season results (Instagram and Facebook): 1M+ views, 44K accounts reached (+24%), 24.2K interactions (+93.8%), +28.2% follower growth, +162% watch time.
- Lessons: consistency wins; sponsors need stories; build, then scale.
- Stack: Canva, Lightroom, Sony α6400 + SEL70350G, Google Forms.
