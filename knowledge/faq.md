# Frequent questions (validated answers, adapt the wording)

**Why digital transformation in sport, after communication and events?**
Because every field project ended the same way: the team was missing a tool, and I ended up building it. At Royal Daring it was a sponsor website and a Notion handover; at ASN95 a prediction game and templates, and later Sifflo, a full app for the club. My thesis now studies what generative AI and no-code can do for clubs with little time and money.

**You don't have a coding background. How do you build?**
I direct AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex). I write the brief, decide the structure, write the content, check every screen and deploy. The agents write most of the code. It lets me ship real tools, and it is exactly the kind of workflow small organisations can adopt.

**What kind of internship are you looking for?**
Digital transformation and AI in a sport organisation: club, federation, league, event organiser or sport-tech company. February to June 2027, in France, Belgium or elsewhere in Europe. It is the final internship of my ISA master's.

**What would you do in your first weeks?**
Listen and map. Who does what, where time is lost, which tasks repeat every week. Then propose one small tool or automation that saves time quickly, and make sure the team can run it without me.
